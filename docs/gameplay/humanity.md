# Humanity

Humanity is a rare secondary currency used to reverse hollowing and kindle bonfires for extra Estus charges.

---

## Earning Humanity

Humanity drops as a rare reward from defeating enemies. Not all enemies drop it, and even those that do only have a small chance:

| Enemy Type | Humanity Drop Chance |
|---|---|
| Dragons | 10% |
| Void creatures | 10% |
| Critters | 10% |
| Golems | 5% |
| Undead | 5% |
| Intelligent (Trorks, Goblins, etc.) | 3% |
| Default (unmatched enemies) | 2% |
| Spirits | 1% |
| Beasts | 1% |
| Wildlife, Livestock, Flying, Aquatic, Passive | No drop |

You can also find **Concentrated Humanity** items. Right-click to consume them and gain 1 Humanity.

---

## Using Humanity

### Reverse Hollowing

When you die, you become [hollow](death). To restore your human form, rest at a bonfire and select **Reverse Hollowing**. This costs humanity (consumed automatically).

### Kindle Bonfires

Kindling a bonfire increases how many Estus charges you receive when resting there. Each kindle level costs 1 Humanity. You must be in human form (not hollow) to kindle.

See [Bonfires](bonfires) for kindle level details.

---

## Losing Humanity

When you die, **all humanity is lost**. Unlike consumable soul items, there's no way to bank humanity -- what you have is what's at risk.

---

## Checking Your Humanity

- Use `/humanitycount` to see your current humanity total
- The on-screen humanity HUD displays your count (see [HUD Customization](../hud))
