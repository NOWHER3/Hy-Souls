# Death & Hollowing

Death in Hy-Souls carries real consequences, just like in Dark Souls.

---

## What Happens When You Die

When you die:

1. **All souls are lost** -- your soul counter is set to zero
2. **All humanity is lost** -- your humanity counter is set to zero
3. **You become hollow** -- your character's appearance changes
4. **Items drop at your death location** -- certain items fall where you died

After dying, you remain in the death pose until you click **Respawn**.

---

## Respawning

When you click Respawn:

- If you've rested at a bonfire, you **teleport to the last bonfire you rested at**
- If you haven't rested at any bonfire, you respawn at the **default spawn point**
- If the bonfire you rested at has been **destroyed**, your respawn point is cleared and you use the default spawn

---

## Hollowing

After dying, your character becomes **hollow**, visually changing your player model to a withered appearance.

Being hollow has one key restriction: **you cannot kindle bonfires while hollow**. To kindle, you must first reverse your hollowing.

### Reversing Hollowing

To restore your human form:

1. Rest at any bonfire
2. Select **Reverse Hollowing** from the bonfire menu
3. This consumes Humanity from your counter

Once reversed, you return to your normal appearance and regain the ability to kindle bonfires.

{: .note }
> You'll become hollow again the next time you die, so make sure to use your humanity wisely.

---

## Items That Drop on Death

The following items are dropped at your death location:

- Consumable soul items (Soul of a Skeleton Undead, etc.)
- Humanity Essence
- Concentrated Humanity
- Homeward Bones
- Metal bars crafted at the bonfire

Items that are **not** dropped on death:

- Estus Flask (stays with you)
