# Hy-Souls Wiki

**Hy-Souls** is a Dark Souls-inspired mod for Hytale that brings the punishing-but-rewarding gameplay loop of the Souls series into the world of Hytale.

---

## Features at a Glance

- **Souls** -- Earn souls by defeating enemies and finding them in chests. Spend them at bonfires to craft gear, trade for materials, and more.
- **Humanity** -- A rare secondary currency that lets you reverse hollowing and kindle bonfires.
- **Bonfires** -- Craftable rest points where you respawn, recharge your Estus, craft items, and kindle for extra healing charges.
- **Estus Flask** -- A rechargeable healing flask that refills every time you rest at a bonfire.
- **Homeward Bone** -- A consumable that teleports you back to the last bonfire you rested at.
- **Death Penalty** -- When you die, you lose all your souls and humanity, turn hollow, and drop items at your death location.
- **Hollowing** -- Die and you become hollow, changing your appearance. Reverse it at a bonfire using humanity.
- **Chest Loot** -- Dungeon and world chests can contain soul items of varying value.
- **HUD Display** -- On-screen counters for your soul and humanity totals, fully customizable.

---

## Getting Started

1. [Install the mod](server-guide/installation)
2. Craft a [Bonfire](gameplay/bonfires) and place it in the world
3. Right-click the bonfire to rest, set your spawn, and start crafting
4. Head out, defeat enemies, and collect [Souls](gameplay/souls)
