# Estus Flask

The Estus Flask is a rechargeable healing item that restores health instantly and provides health regeneration over time.

---

## How It Works

- Use the Estus Flask to apply **Instant Heal III** and **Health Regen III**
- You start with **5 charges** (can be increased by kindling bonfires)
- When all charges are used, the flask becomes an **Estus (Empty)** with no effect
- Resting at any bonfire **fully recharges** your Estus Flask
- The Estus Flask is **not dropped on death** -- it stays in your inventory

---

## Increasing Charges

You can increase your Estus capacity by **kindling** a bonfire. Each kindle level adds 5 more charges:

| Kindle Level | Estus Charges |
|---|---|
| 0 (default) | 5 |
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 15 |
| 3 (max) | 20 |

See [Bonfires - Kindling](../gameplay/bonfires#kindling) for details.

---

## Hotbar Slot

By default, the Estus Flask is placed in your hotbar automatically. You can change which slot it goes to:

```
/estusslot <1-9>
```

For example, `/estusslot 1` places it in the first hotbar slot. This preference is saved and persists across sessions.

---

## Max Stack

You can carry up to **5 Estus charges** at base level (up to 20 with full kindling).
