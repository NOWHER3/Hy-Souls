# Homeward Bone

The Homeward Bone is a consumable item that teleports you back to the last bonfire you rested at.

---

## How It Works

1. Hold the Homeward Bone in your hand
2. Right-click to use it
3. You are instantly teleported to the last bonfire you rested at
4. One Homeward Bone is consumed per use

---

## Obtaining

- **Purchase at a bonfire** -- 150 Souls, 2 second craft time (Merchant tab)
- **Find in chests** -- Can appear as loot in dungeon and world chests

---

## Important Notes

- Max stack size: **5**
- **Drops on death** -- Homeward Bones in your inventory will fall at your death location
- If you haven't rested at a bonfire yet, you'll see: *"No bonfire rested at"*
- If the bonfire you rested at has been destroyed: *"Bonfire no longer exists"*
- Cross-world teleportation is not supported: *"Cannot teleport across worlds"*
