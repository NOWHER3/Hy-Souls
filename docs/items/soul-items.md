# Soul Items

Soul items are consumable treasures found in chests throughout the world. Unlike Soul Essence dropped by enemies (which is auto-consumed on pickup), these items stay in your inventory until you choose to use them.

---

## Consumable Soul Items

Right-click any of these items to consume them and add their soul value to your counter.

| Item | Souls Gained |
|---|---|
| Soul of a Skeleton Undead | 200 |
| Large Soul of a Skeleton Undead | 400 |
| Soul of a nameless Goblin | 800 |
| Large Soul of a nameless Goblin | 1,000 |
| Soul of a proud Slothian | 2,000 |
| Large Soul of a proud Slothian | 3,000 |
| Soul of a brave Feran | 5,000 |
| Large Soul of a brave Feran | 8,000 |
| Soul of a Hero Kweebec | 10,000 |
| Large Soul of a Hero Kweebec | 20,000 |

---

## Where to Find Them

Soul items appear in **dungeon chests, world chests, and other lootable containers** placed by the world generator. The rarity and value of soul items found depends on the type of chest:

- **Common chests** (villages, camps, cabins) -- lower-value soul items
- **Rare chests** (temples, ruins, fortresses) -- mid-value soul items
- **Epic chests** (boss rooms, vaults, dungeons) -- high-value soul items
- **Legendary chests** (treasure rooms) -- the most valuable soul items

Player-placed chests are **never** populated with loot.

---

## Strategy Tips

- Soul items act as a **bank** -- they hold value without risking your soul counter
- However, they **drop on death**, so they're not completely safe
- Consider consuming them before a boss fight to spend the souls on gear
- Or save them for when you need a large soul investment at a bonfire
