# Souls

Souls are the primary currency in Hy-Souls. You earn them by defeating enemies and finding soul items in chests, and spend them at bonfires to craft gear and trade for materials.

---

## Earning Souls

### Defeating Enemies

When you kill an NPC, it drops **Soul Essence** on the ground. Walk over it to pick it up -- the item is automatically consumed and added to your soul counter.

Different enemies drop different amounts of souls:

| Enemy Type | Souls Dropped |
|---|---|
| Dragons | 1,500 |
| Void creatures | 1,500 |
| Golems | 600 |
| Undead (Skeletons, Zombies) | 600 |
| Intelligent (Trorks, Outlanders, Goblins, Kweebec, Feran) | 300 |
| Spirits | 150 |
| Beasts (Fen Stalkers, Spiders, Scorpions, Warthogs) | 150 |
| Wildlife (Deer, Moose, Bison, Ram) | 50 |
| Livestock (Pigs, Sheep, Cows, Horses) | 30 |
| Aquatic creatures | 25 |
| Critters (Mice, Rats, Rabbits) | 20 |
| Flying critters (Birds, Chickens, Turkeys) | 15 |
| Passive creatures | 10 |

### Finding Soul Items in Chests

Dungeon and world chests can contain **consumable soul items**. Unlike regular soul essence, these stay in your inventory until you choose to use them. Right-click to consume and add the souls to your counter.

| Item | Souls Gained |
|---|---|
| Soul of a Skeleton Undead | 200 |
| Large Soul of a Skeleton Undead | 400 |
| Soul of a nameless Goblin | 800 |
| Large Soul of a nameless Goblin | 1,000 |
| Soul of a proud Slothian | 2,000 |
| Large Soul of a proud Slothian | 3,000 |
| Soul of a brave Feran | 5,000 |
| Large Soul of a brave Feran | 8,000 |
| Soul of a Hero Kweebec | 10,000 |
| Large Soul of a Hero Kweebec | 20,000 |

{: .warning }
> Consumable soul items **drop on death**, so consider using them before heading into dangerous areas -- or save them as a bank for later.

---

## Spending Souls

You spend souls at a [Bonfire](bonfires) through its crafting menu:

- **Soul Essence Exchange** -- Trade souls for metal bars (Copper, Iron, Silver, Thorium, Cobalt, Gold, Adamantite, Mithril)
- **Armor Crafting** -- Craft the Solaire armor set using souls and materials
- **Merchant** -- Purchase consumables like Homeward Bones

See [Bonfires](bonfires) for full crafting details.

---

## Checking Your Souls

- Use `/soulcount` to see your current soul total
- The on-screen soul HUD displays your count at all times (see [HUD Customization](../hud))
